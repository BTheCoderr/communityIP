Quick answers for editing the website at /admin

Form submissions (contact, apply, volunteer, partner) are not edited here. Those go to Netlify Forms in the site dashboard.

Sign in

  1. Open /admin on the live site.
  2. Click Login with Netlify Identity.
  3. Use the email and password from your Netlify invite.

Edit a page

  1. Choose a collection on the left (Home Page, About Page, etc.).
  2. Change the text or image fields.
  3. Click Save (draft) or Publish when ready.

Update the logo or an image

  • Site logo: Site Settings → Logo → upload → Publish.
  • Hero or news images: open the page or post → Image field → upload.
  • Uploaded files are saved to the site automatically after Publish.

Leadership & board

  • Co-Presidents — names, photos, bios, order.
  • Board Members — same fields for directors.

News posts

  1. News Posts → New News Posts.
  2. Fill title, date, excerpt, and body.
  3. Publish when ready.

Drafts vs. published

  • Save keeps a draft for review.
  • Publish sends changes to the live website (usually within a few minutes).

Sign out

Use the account menu in the top-right of the admin screen → Log out.
